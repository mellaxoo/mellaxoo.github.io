Mellaxoo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Mellaxoo provides digital healthcare and appointment-management services that help patients, hospitals, clinics, and healthcare professionals manage appointments and related communications.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how Mellaxoo is used, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Age and gender
  • Hospital or clinic information
  • Doctor information
  • Appointment dates and times
  • Appointment status and related details
  • Information voluntarily provided while using Mellaxoo services

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage patient and hospital accounts
  • Book, reschedule, cancel, and manage appointments
  • Connect patients with hospitals, clinics, and doctors
  • Send appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Send doctor assignment or appointment change notifications
  • Provide patient and hospital support
  • Maintain the security and reliability of Mellaxoo
  • Improve our services and user experience

3. WhatsApp Communications

Mellaxoo may use WhatsApp Business services provided by Meta to send service-related messages such as appointment confirmations, reminders, rescheduling notifications, cancellation notifications, reactivation notifications, and doctor assignment updates.

Information required to deliver these communications, such as a phone number and appointment details, may be processed through Meta and WhatsApp in accordance with their applicable terms and privacy policies.

4. Information Sharing

Mellaxoo does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared where necessary with:

  • Hospitals and clinics involved in an appointment
  • Doctors and healthcare professionals involved in an appointment
  • Technology and infrastructure service providers
  • Communication providers such as Meta and WhatsApp
  • Authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law

5. Data Security

Mellaxoo uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss, or disclosure.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Mellaxoo services, maintain records, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the platform.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Users may contact Mellaxoo to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.
